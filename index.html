Good to know

Questions, answered.

Not sure what your business needs yet? That's okay. We can start with the goals and work out the right scope together.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly the content and feedback are ready. A simple site can take much less time than a larger multi-page project. We'll agree on a realistic timeline before work begins.

What do you need from me?

Usually your business name, services, preferred contact details, any logo or brand assets you have, and examples of websites or styles you like. If you don't have everything ready, we'll identify what's missing.

Will the website work on phones?

Responsive layouts are part of the approach, so the site is designed to adapt to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

Can you help after launch?

Post-launch updates and ongoing support can be discussed as part of the project. The agreed scope will state what's included and what would be quoted separately.

Do I need to understand web development?

No. We'll explain decisions in plain language, keep the process clear, and ask for your input on the business details and content only you can provide.
